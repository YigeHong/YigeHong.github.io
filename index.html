Research interests

I study decision making under uncertainty, with an emphasis on large stochastic systems. My work combines mathematical modeling, algorithm design, and performance analysis. My research interests include:

Queueing and scheduling: I study queueing performance, resource allocation, and scheduling in large service systems. My work includes multiserver queues and stochastic bin packing, motivated by applications in data centers and emerging workloads such as large language model inference.

Decision-making and learning: I use methods from stochastic processes to design and analyze algorithms for stochastic decision problems, including restless bandits and weakly coupled Markov decision processes. My interests also include reinforcement learning.
